# Silicon Labs C2 transport

`c2` samples C2D on rising C2CK. A measured C2CK low interval of at least 20 µs
establishes reset and synchronization; a known positive sample rate is required.
The default waits for this reset. `start_aligned` explicitly permits a capture
starting immediately before a START pulse. After a gap, recovery always requires
an observed reset.

The decoder recognizes the two-bit LSB-first instructions Data Read, Data Write,
Address Read and Address Write. Data instructions carry a two-bit length plus one
(1–4 bytes). Bytes stream LSB-first with their address/data role and direction.
Read WAIT occurs before data; write WAIT after data. WAIT zeros are polled until
one, rather than treated as a fixed-width field. `max_wait_cycles` defaults to
1024 and bounds the counter; reaching it emits `wait-limit` and requires reset.
Address instructions transfer one byte without a data WAIT field. The final STOP
pulse completes a frame. Reset, gaps and EOF abort unfinished frames.

This transport does not infer flash programming commands, protected state,
electrical contention, debug-register contents or nonvolatile success. It keeps
one unfinished byte, fixed counters and provenance, never a complete transaction.

Reference: [Silicon Labs AN127, Flash Programming via the C2 Interface](https://www.silabs.com/documents/public/application-notes/AN127.pdf), sections 1.2–1.5.
The implementation and synthetic vectors are independently authored from this
public manufacturer specification.
