# AM230x

The readings of an AM2302 (DHT22) or DHT11 humidity and temperature sensor, decoded from the logic samples of its single data line. Node type `am230x`; module `crates/uiharu-protocols/src/am230x/`. Implemented from the Aosong AM2302 (DHT22) and DHT11 datasheets.

The open-drain line idles high. The host pulls it low for at least 1 ms (18 ms for the DHT11) and releases it; 20 to 40 µs later (10 to 200 µs on an AM2302) the sensor answers with 80 µs low and 80 µs high, then sends 40 bits — each a 50 µs low followed by a high of 26 to 28 µs for a 0 or 70 µs for a 1 — and ends with a 50 µs low before releasing the line. The 40 bits are five bytes, most significant bit first: two of humidity, two of temperature and a checksum.

## Configuration

| Field       | Default  | Meaning                                                                                                     |
| ----------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `data`      | 0        | The data line's channel                                                                                     |
| `model`     | `am2302` | `am2302` (also for the DHT22) or `dht11`: the shortest start signal and when the sensor answers its release |
| `tolerance` | 30       | How far a pulse may stray from the datasheet's time, in percent, 0 to 40                                    |

The tolerance is at most 40 %, which keeps the windows of a 0 and a 1 apart (28 µs + 40 % is still shorter than 70 µs − 40 %). The model does not change how the bytes are reported, only how they are read (below).

The input port `logic` requires of the source `channels >= data + 1` and `rateHz >= 1000000`: a sample per microsecond. The output port `events` declares `model` (`"am2302"` or `"dht11"`).

## The stream: `am230x.events` v1

The stream is unframed: a reading is one event, decided at the sensor's final release. An exchange that went wrong is an `error` instead.

| Kind      | Fields                                                                          | Meaning                                                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reading` | `bytes: [u8; 5]`, `checksumOk: bool`, `humidityRaw: u16`, `temperatureRaw: u16` | The five bytes the sensor sent and the two registers they hold                     |
| `error`   | `reason`: `start`, `response`, `bit` or `count`                                 | An exchange that could not be read; pulses are skipped up to the next start signal |

- `checksumOk` says whether the fifth byte is the sum of the first four, modulo 256.
- `humidityRaw` is the first two bytes as a 16-bit number, high byte first; `temperatureRaw` the next two. They are the sensor's registers, not values in units:

  | Model          | Humidity                                         | Temperature                                                                           |
  | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | AM2302 (DHT22) | `humidityRaw` / 10 % RH                          | (`temperatureRaw` & 0x7fff) / 10 °C, negative when bit 15 is set (sign and magnitude) |
  | DHT11          | high byte: integral % RH, low byte: decimal part | high byte: integral °C, low byte: decimal part                                        |

- `start`: a low pulse on an idle line that is no start signal — shorter than the model's start signal, such as a sensor's response or bits whose start signal was not seen. `response`: after a start signal, the host's release, the sensor's response low or its response high fell outside its window. `bit`: a bit's low or high, or the final low, fell outside its window. `count`: the line went idle before the fortieth bit — a bit's high outlasted the window of a 1.

```json
{"kind":"reading","bytes":[2,140,1,95,238],"checksumOk":true,"humidityRaw":652,"temperatureRaw":351}
{"kind":"error","reason":"response"}
```

| Event     | Span                                                                               | Settled                                                                                 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reading` | the start signal's fall through the sensor's final release                         | the final release                                                                       |
| `error`   | the exchange from its start signal (or the pulse without one) through the decision | the edge that ended a pulse too short, or the last tick of the window a pulse outlasted |

Progress never passes the start signal of a reading under way, nor the fall of a low that may yet turn out to be a start signal: after progress at tick _t_, no event begins before _t_.

## Decoding rules

- Every pulse is measured from the edge that began it to the edge that ended it, and must lie in its window: the datasheet's time widened by `tolerance` percent, the shortest bound rounded up to whole ticks and the longest down — ⌈_t_ · (100 − tolerance) · rate / 10⁸⌉ and ⌊_t_ · (100 + tolerance) · rate / 10⁸⌋. The model sets the start signal, which has a shortest bound only, and the host's release; the other windows are the same for both.

  | Pulse                       | Datasheet      | Window at 30 %   |
  | --------------------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
  | start signal (AM2302)       | at least 1 ms  | at least 700 µs  |
  | start signal (DHT11)        | at least 18 ms | at least 12.6 ms |
  | host's release (AM2302)     | 10 to 200 µs   | 7 to 260 µs      |
  | host's release (DHT11)      | 20 to 40 µs    | 14 to 52 µs      |
  | response low, response high | 80 µs          | 56 to 104 µs     |
  | bit low, final low          | 50 µs          | 35 to 65 µs      |
  | high of a 0                 | 26 to 28 µs    | 18.2 to 36.4 µs  |
  | high of a 1                 | 70 µs          | 49 to 91 µs      |

- A low at least as long as a start signal begins a reading, wherever it comes; a shorter low on an idle line is a `start` error at its release.
- After the start signal come the host's release, the response low and high, the 40 bits and the final low, each in its window. A pulse outside its window ends the reading — `response` or `bit` —, decided at the edge that ended it when it was too short, and at the window's last tick when it outlasted it. A bit's high between the windows of a 0 and a 1 is a `bit` error; one that outlasts the window of a 1 is a `count` error.
- After an error the pulses are skipped up to the next start signal.
- A low found at the first sample, or at the first after a gap, began unseen: it is a start signal only if what was seen of it is already long enough, and the reading then spans from the first sample seen; otherwise the decoder waits for the next start signal.
- A reading cut off by a gap or the end of the samples is not reported: the stream has no frame to abort. The gap or the end is passed on, and what the cut-off reading — or a low under way at the gap — leaves on the line after the gap is skipped up to the next start signal; a line that was idle when the gap began is idle after it.

## Limits

- Apart from the host's release, the windows hold the datasheets' typical values; a sensor that strays further needs a wider tolerance.
- A start signal has no longest bound: a line held low for long, then released, starts a reading.
- The registers are reported raw; scaling them to units is the reader's, by the table above.

## Engineering-value consumer

`am230x-values` is an original TypeScript stream consumer of the same canonical
`am230x.events@1` contract. Its `model` is `am2302` (default, also DHT22) or classic
`dht11`. It reports integer milli-percent relative humidity and milli-Celsius
magnitude with a separate `negative` flag. AM2302's temperature is sign-magnitude;
classic DHT11 requires zero decimal bytes. A failed checksum or unsupported DHT11
decimal representation yields an error, without an engineering reading. Values
outside the AM2302 envelope retain `valid: false` and receive a range error.

Conversions follow the manufacturer-authored [Aosong AM2302 technical manual](https://www.aosong.com/uploadfiles/2025/04/20250417105409216.pdf).
The native producer keeps its signal algorithm and exact public `[u8; 5]` payload,
now generated from `dsl/contracts/am230x.ts`; both four and six-byte JSON arrays
remain rejected. Native/generated/interpreted value, sign, checksum, classic
DHT11 and scheduling vectors are in `uiharu-interp/tests/p4_devices.rs`.
